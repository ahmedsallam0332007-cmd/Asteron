# Impact

**Who it helps**
- Crews beyond low Earth orbit (Moon, Mars), who will face medical problems without real-time help from Earth (NASA HRP Risk 15, Earth-independent operations).
- Flight surgeons, who see each astronaut with the age of the data and never mistake old data for current.
- On Earth: remote clinics, ships, desert and polar stations, and any team where the network comes and goes.

**What is new**
- Health monitoring that knows the delay: it computes when Earth can answer and, if that is too late, tells the crew to act on board now.
- Each astronaut is compared with their own normal, and physical and psychological stress are tracked together.
- Every report is sealed and nothing is lost during weeks of blackout.

**Why it is credible**
- Built on NASA's own data and limits (JPL Horizons, OSDR OSD-569, NASA-STD-3001, NASA NTRS), honest about what is simulated.
- Measured: 381 tests, 200 / 200 urgent cases caught, 0.25% false alerts, 52 / 52 attacks refused, 15 days of Mars blackout handled on board.

**Next steps**
Clinical review of every threshold, real wearable input (a smart shirt of the CSA Bio-Monitor class), a Bundle Protocol v7 transport, an Arabic interface, and trials in analogue missions.
